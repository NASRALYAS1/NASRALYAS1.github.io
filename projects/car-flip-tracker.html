Car Flip Tracker

Finance for a car-trading business

An app that runs a two-partner car-trading business: what every car cost, what it sold for, who still owes instalments, and each partner's share of the profit, with money handled in US dollars and Iraqi dinars at once.

The problem

Two partners buy and sell cars together. A single car can be bought in dollars, repaired in dinars, sold on instalments over a year, or traded for another car that is then sold again. Working out the real profit, and who owes whom, by hand quickly turns into arguments.

The app had to keep every number traceable back to the original entry, in the currency it was actually paid in, and give both partners the same answer.

What I built

  • Every car's full cost: purchase price plus each expense, with one-tap presets for the common ones like fuel and car washes.
  • Cash and instalment sales, down payments, planned monthly instalments, partial payments and final settlements with a discount.
  • Car-for-car trades followed as a full chain, so the profit lands on the right car at the end.
  • Two currencies, done properly: every amount keeps its original currency and that day's exchange rate, so old records never change when the rate moves.
  • Partner shares: custom profit splits, profit distributions, and debts between the partners with partial repayments.
  • Overdue reminders: push notifications to the partners when an instalment is late, and an optional WhatsApp reminder to the buyer.
  • Photos of each car and an automatic daily backup of all data to cloud storage.
  • Security: hashed passwords, lock-out after repeated failed logins, a recovery code, and an optional PIN or fingerprint lock on each phone.
  • Safe by design: shared records only change while online, so two partners can never create conflicting copies of the same payment. Personal debts still work offline and sync later.
