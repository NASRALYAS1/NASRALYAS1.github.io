Khan Market

Point of sale & retail management

A complete point-of-sale and retail-management system, in real daily use by a market. Barcode sales, stock, customer credit, suppliers, cash shifts and staff, all in one Arabic desktop app.

the app in use

The problem

A market sells all day, often on credit, with several staff handling cash. The owner needs to know what is selling, what is running low, who owes what, and whether the till balances at the end of every shift, without adding it up by hand.

The system had to be fast at the counter, work in Arabic, keep running whatever the internet is doing, and never lose the shop's records.

What I built

  • Barcode point of sale with category filters, quick search by name, and sales that can be held and resumed.
  • Stock management: purchase price, sale price and profit per item, low-stock alerts, total stock value, import from a file and barcode label printing.
  • Product-expiry tracking, suppliers and purchases.
  • Customer credit ledgers with overdue tracking at 30, 60 and 90 days and one-click WhatsApp reminders.
  • Daily cash shifts and a cash drawer, invoices, and 80 mm thermal-printer receipts.
  • Employees with attendance and payroll, and multiple users with roles and an activity log.
  • Dashboard and reports on sales, profit and debts.
  • Automatic local and off-machine backups, plus a data-integrity check that reconciles stock, invoices and debts.
