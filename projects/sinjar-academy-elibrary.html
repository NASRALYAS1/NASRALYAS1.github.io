The problem

Sinjar Academy holds thousands of books, many donated by BookAid, from primary-school readers to postgraduate texts. Readers needed a way to find out what the library holds, and whether a book is free, before travelling to the centre in Sinuni.

The site had to work for readers of all ages, in English and Arabic, on phones as well as computers.

What I built

  • A searchable catalogue of over 2,700 titles across 13 genres, with live available / unavailable status on every book.
  • Book details, including author, language and description, with a link to more information from Google.
  • Membership and login, so registered readers can request a book online.
  • Borrowing rules built in: one book at a time, a 24-hour hold to collect it, and up to 40 days to keep it.
  • Full English and Arabic versions, switched with one tap, with the layout mirrored for Arabic.
  • Responsive design that works on phones as well as computers.
  • Instructions and contact pages, including the centre's location on Google Maps.
